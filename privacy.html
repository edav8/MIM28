Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

This site is a personal, non-commercial study aid made by a student of the INSEAD Master in Management, class of 2028. It is not an INSEAD website and INSEAD has no involvement in it.

The short version

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, contains no advertising, has no login, and makes no requests to any third-party server. It asks you for nothing and stores nothing about you anywhere except in your own browser, where only you can read it.

Who is responsible

The site is operated by an individual student, who is the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Because the site is hosted in the European Union context and its visitors are mostly in France, it is written to comply with the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive.

To be completed before publication

A privacy policy must name a contact through which data subjects can exercise their rights. Replace this box with a contact address you are willing to publish. A dedicated alias is usually wiser than a personal student address, because anything published here will be harvested by scrapers.

What this site collects

Nothing is sent to us. There is no server that belongs to this site, no database, and no form that transmits anything. Specifically:

  • No cookies of any kind, first-party or third-party.
  • No analytics or measurement — no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Matomo, no pixels, no beacons.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email collection, no contact form.
  • No third-party requests. Fonts, the JavaScript libraries and the background video are all served from this site's own address, so no outside company is told that you visited.

What is stored in your browser

The site remembers your progress so that a drill you finished stays finished. This uses your browser's localStorage, which is not a cookie and is never transmitted anywhere. It holds things like which drills you completed, your best scores, which to-do items you ticked, and which page you were last on.

This data stays on the device you are using. It is not readable by this site's operator, by INSEAD, or by anyone else. It is not synced between your devices. Clearing your browser's site data for this address deletes it permanently, and the site keeps working — you simply start from a blank slate.

Under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive, storage that is strictly necessary to provide a service the user has explicitly requested does not require consent. Remembering the progress of the trainer you are using is exactly that, which is why you are not shown a consent banner. See the Cookies Policy for the detail.

The one thing outside our control: hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (a Microsoft company). Like any web server, GitHub's servers receive and may log technical information when you request a page — typically your IP address, the time, the page requested, and your browser's user-agent string. This is standard server operation and it happens before any of this site's own code runs; we neither control it nor receive the logs.

GitHub is based in the United States, so this constitutes a transfer of personal data outside the European Economic Area. GitHub relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses for such transfers. GitHub's own privacy statement governs that processing and is published at docs.github.com.

If that transfer concerns you, the practical answer is that you can read everything on this site through a VPN or Tor without any loss of function, because the site itself never looks at who you are.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL.

In practice these rights are easy to satisfy here, because there is almost nothing to exercise them against. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or erase. The only data that exists is the progress in your own browser, which you can delete yourself at any time through your browser's settings, and the host's server logs, for which you would contact GitHub.

Children

The site is aimed at students on a postgraduate programme. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
