Cookies Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

The short version

This site uses no cookies. Not necessary ones, not functional ones, not analytics ones, not advertising ones. That is also why you have not been shown a consent banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Why there is no banner

Consent banners exist because Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive requires consent before storing information on a user's device — with one exception. Consent is not required where the storage is strictly necessary to provide a service the user has explicitly requested.

This site sets no cookies at all, and the one thing it does store falls squarely inside that exception. So a banner would be theatre: it would ask permission for something that needs none, and train you to click through the ones that do.

What is stored, precisely

The trainer keeps your progress using localStorage, a browser feature that is related to cookies but behaves differently in the way that matters: it is never attached to network requests. A cookie is sent to a server with every page you load. localStorage is not sent anywhere, ever. It sits in your browser and only code running on this site's own address can read it.

What is deliberately absent

  • No analytics. The operator of this site genuinely does not know how many people visit it, which is a fair price for not tracking anyone.
  • No third-party requests. Fonts, JavaScript libraries and video are all served from this site's own address. Loading fonts from Google, for example, would send your IP address to Google — which a Munich court held in January 2022 to breach the GDPR. Self-hosting removes that entirely.
  • No embeds. No YouTube player, no social widgets, no comment system, no chat widget, no maps. Each of those would reintroduce a third party and, with it, a consent requirement.

How to delete it

Clearing your browser's site data for this address removes everything instantly and permanently. The trainer then behaves as it does for a first-time visitor.

  • Chrome — click the icon to the left of the address bar, then Cookies and site data → Manage on-device site data → delete.
  • Safari — Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data, search for the site, remove.
  • Firefox — click the padlock → Clear cookies and site data.
  • Any browser — a private or incognito window stores nothing beyond the session.

If you use the site in a private window, your progress will not survive closing it. That is the trade, and it is yours to make.
